About Me

I'm Mayank Chhatrola, a UI/UX Designer with a background in Android development and a passion for creating intuitive digital experiences.

My journey into design began while developing mobile applications, where I realized that great products are not just built with code - they're shaped by the user's experience. This curiosity led me to explore UI/UX design, where I discovered my passion for solving problems through clean, functional, and visually appealing interfaces.

I enjoy transforming ideas into user-friendly experiences through research, wireframing, prototyping, and visual design. With hands-on experience in Figma and a strong understanding of development workflows, I design solutions that are both user-centered and practical to implement.

Currently, I'm pursuing opportunities to work on mobile applications (iOS/Android) and immersive digital experiences that challenge me to learn, grow and create meaningful impact through design.

Design Process

Empathize 01

I start by learning about the target users, their goals, pain points, and expectations to ensure every design decision solves a real problem.

Research 02

I analyze competitors, study user behaviors, and gather market insights to identify opportunities and improve the overall experience.

Define 03

After research, I define user needs, project objectives, and key problems that the design must solve.

Wireframe 04

I create low-fidelity wireframes to establish layout, information hierarchy, and user flows before focusing on details.

Prototype 05

Using Figma, I build interactive prototypes that simulate real user interaction and validate design concepts early.

Test 06

I evaluate usability, gather feedback, and identify friction points to understand how users interact with the design.

Improve 07

Based on insights and feedback, I iterate on the design to improve usability, accessibility, and visual consistency.
